# The murmuration's bird

- The pigeon: a photogrammetry scan of a museum study skin, "Rock Pigeon - Columba livia" (MLZ:Bird:65962),
  by MooreLab — the Moore Laboratory of Zoology, Occidental College —
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/rock-pigeon-columba-livia-12fac1c431644b7f9480079636aee332, CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Fetched from the Objaverse mirror of Sketchfab's CC models
  (https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse). Cleaned, decimated to three levels of detail and
  re-textured from its own texture here (tools/show-flock/pigeon_build.py). A study skin has its wings
  folded, so the wings are modelled, textured from the scan's own feathers, and hinged at the shoulders.
