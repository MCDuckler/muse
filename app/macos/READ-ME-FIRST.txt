WetOwl for Mac

Drag WetOwl onto Applications, then open it from there.

The first time, macOS says it cannot check WetOwl for malicious software and
will not open it: this build is not signed by a paid Apple developer account.
To allow it, once:

  Open WetOwl (it is refused), then System Settings > Privacy & Security,
  scroll down to "WetOwl was blocked", click Open Anyway, then Open, and
  give your password.

Or, in Terminal:   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/WetOwl.app

After that it opens like any other app, and updates itself (Settings > About).
Keep it in Applications: opened from the disk image or from Downloads, macOS
runs it from a read-only copy and it cannot update itself.

Fetching music for the house and taking records apart for the booth
(Settings > This computer) want yt-dlp, ffmpeg and deno. With Homebrew
(https://brew.sh), in Terminal:

  brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg deno

WetOwl finds them there by itself. Taking records apart runs on Apple silicon;
an Intel Mac leaves that to the other computers in the house.
